Rate sheet format

One row per charge. Use * in origin, destination or carrier to apply a charge to all of them; a more specific row overrides a wildcard row with the same charge_code. Every option needs a FRT row with a specific carrier.

ColumnValues
modeAIR, LCL, FCL
categoryORIGIN, FREIGHT, DEST, IMPORT_CLEARANCE, DELIVERY
basisKG (chargeable kg for air, gross kg for sea) · WM (LCL revenue ton) · CBM · CONTAINER (FCL, per box) · SHIPMENT (flat) · PCT_FRT (% of the FRT line)
containerFCL only: 20GP, 40GP, 40HC, or blank for all container types
break_minWeight/quantity break. The highest break not exceeding the shipment quantity applies (e.g. air +45 / +100 / +300 kg)
min_chargeMinimum amount for the line
currency3-letter code; converted with the FX table in Settings
valid_from / valid_toYYYY-MM-DD, optional. Expired rows are ignored; rows expiring within 14 days are flagged
